Research & Projects

MolScience Platform

2026.04 — Present

Project lead · Open molecular database and chemical data infrastructure

A chemistry data project for the AI era, focused on standardizing, organizing, searching, managing, and applying molecular data. It began as the chemical database initiative within ChemAI and later developed into the independent MolScience project.

  • Lead project planning, database architecture, and technical direction.
  • Design PostgreSQL data models for molecules, properties, data sources, users, permissions, and logs.
  • Study SMILES, InChI, InChIKey, molecular standardization, and unique identifiers; use RDKit for molecular structure processing and chemical information tasks.
  • Build and maintain backend services and server infrastructure using FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and Nginx.
  • Advance the design of molecular and structure search, data quality control, and database APIs.
